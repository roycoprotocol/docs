title: "For Issuers"
---

**Reach entirely new investor segments.** Liquidity-sensitive users can access Senior, yield-seeking users can access Junior, and market-making users can access the SLP Tranche. 

**Increase TVL without changing the strategy.** A single yield source serves multiple risk profiles simultaneously. 

**Improve capital efficiency.** Royco Day lets your liquidity sleeve actively earn yield and fees rather than sitting idle.

**Offer one of the most competitive yield products in the market.** Junior creates yield amplification without relying on leverage loops or carry trades. 

**Native liquidity.** Previously illiquid yield sources can now be distributed through liquid on chain vaults, opening up composability and TVL.

**Potential native integration.** The liquidity layer can be embedded directly into the vault experience, rather than existing as an external wrapper.

**Maximum depositor protection.** The Observation Period ensures your Junior depositors don't get burned by temporary market fluctuations, only taking losses in a true sustained NAV drawdown.
