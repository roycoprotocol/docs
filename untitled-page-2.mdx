---
title: "For Issuers"
---

**Reach entirely new investor segments.** Liquidity or risk-sensitive users can access Senior, while yield-seeking users can access the Junior or SLP Tranches. 

**Increase TVL without changing the strategy.** A single yield source serves multiple risk profiles simultaneously. 

**Offer one of the most competitive yield products in the market.** Junior creates yield amplification without relying on leverage loops or carry trades. Additionally the Observation Period ensures they only take losses in true sustained NAV drawdowns, not temporary market fluctuations.

**DeFi liquidity.** Previously illiquid yield sources can now be distributed through liquid on chain vaults, opening up composability and TVL.

**Potential native integration.** The liquidity layer can be embedded directly into the vault, rather than existing as an external wrapper. This also improves capital efficiency by allowing the liquidity sleeve to actively earn yield and fees rather than sitting idle.

**Reduce slippage and unwind costs.** Liquidity comes from the SLP pool instead of vault withdrawals, improving position management.

**Capital Security.** When an issuer deposits into the junior tranche of their own market, they absorb losses before seniors do. The party who knows the asset best is choosing to stand in front of depositors. This deepens the buffer for senior holders, who will be more willing to accept a lower yield and lower the issuer's cost of capital.
