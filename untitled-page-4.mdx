---
title: "For Distribution"
---

**Liquid collateral for previously illiquid assets.** Senior and SLP Tranches transform duration assets into composable on-chain collateral. 

**Higher-quality collateral.** Protected Senior assets justify higher LTVs than the underlying thanks to their improved risk profile. 

**Enable efficient looping.** Users can lever protected assets instead of the underlying, improving capital efficiency and reducing risk. 

**Unlock fixed rates.** Senior Tranches can integrate with yield trading protocols, enabling fixed-income products and fixed-rate collateral. 

**Reduce slippage and unwind costs.** Liquidity comes from the SLP pool instead of vault withdrawals, improving position management.

**Self-balancing liquidity.** Yield share dynamically shifts incentives toward the liquidity pool whenever additional liquidity is needed, allowing the system to rebalance automatically. 

**A new collateral asset.** The SLP Tranche position is a yield-bearing pool token, listable alongside Senior and Junior.
