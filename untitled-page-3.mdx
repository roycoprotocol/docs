---
title: "For LPs"
description: "Choose your yield. Get liquid, protected yield (Senior), high-yield (Junior), or market-making (SLP) exposure, all based on the same underlying strategy."
---

**Senior:** turn locked strategies into liquid positions. Receive liquid, protected exposure to the underlying yield without giving up flexibility.

**SLP can deliver higher returns with lower risk.** By combining Senior exposure with stablecoins, the SLP Tranche earns both the underlying yield and a liquidity premium. This can result in higher returns than the underlying while maintaining a risk profile similar to, or safer than, the underlying itself.

**Junior:** leveraged upside, protected from noise. The Junior Tranche takes the first loss in exchange for the highest yield. Royco's Observation Period means a Junior position is only marked down when a NAV drop is genuinely severe or sustained. 

**Risk is quantified upfront.** Every market's parameters contractually define how stress events are handled, removing uncertainty around downside scenarios. 

**Collateral/DeFi:** Royco tokens are fully composable, so they can be used in DeFi as collateral or to fix rates.
