title: "For LPs"
---

**Buy/Sell duration assets with liquidity.** Senior sells its shares into the pool at any time, so a strategy with a 30-day redemption becomes a liquid asset.

**Choose your yield.** Get liquid, protected yield (Senior), high-yield (Junior), or market-making (SLP) exposure without changing the underlying strategy.

**Risk is quantified upfront.** Every market defines exactly how stress events are handled, removing uncertainty around downside scenarios. 

**SLP can deliver higher returns with lower risk.** By combining Senior exposure with stablecoins, the SLP Tranche earns both the underlying yield and a liquidity premium. This can result in higher returns than the underlying while maintaining a risk profile similar to, or safer than, the underlying itself.

**Higher yields without leverage loops.** The SLP Tranche delivers competitive yields without introducing recursive leverage or negative carry risk.
